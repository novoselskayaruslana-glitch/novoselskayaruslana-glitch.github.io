Brand Brief

The
Brand.

Machi Matcha

A neighborhood matcha café built on community first, no pretension. The name combines 抹茶 (matcha) with the idea of a local patch — your corner of the city.


Tagline: "your corner of calm."


Handle: @drinkmachimatcha


Tone: Warm, unhurried, sincere. Never trendy for the sake of it.


Target: Local community, 22–35, people who want a third place that doesn't perform wellness at them.

Strategic Goals
  • Build brand awareness before doors open — through mood, not announcements
  • Earn community trust by inviting the audience into the story
  • Drive foot traffic on launch day through organic anticipation
  • Seed UGC habits early so customers become creators
  • Build a Pinterest presence that drives long-tail saves and SEO

Strategy

Three
Phases.

Phase 01
Something's Brewing
Days 1–7
No name, no logo. Pure mood and curiosity. Build intrigue before the reveal so the announcement lands harder. The audience knows something is coming — they don't know what.
Phase 02
Open Building
Days 8–14
Name reveal, menu teases, community seeding. Invite the audience to feel part of the story before doors open. Turn followers into invested pre-customers.
Phase 03
We're Open
Days 15–21
Real-time energy, first UGC, gratitude posts. Sustain the launch wave and turn first-timers into regulars. Close the launch chapter and open the "this is your place now" chapter.
